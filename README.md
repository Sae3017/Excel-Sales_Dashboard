## Project Objective
Retail Sales Analysis and Performance Dashboard Using Microsoft Excel
## Dataset used


## Questions  (KPIs)
-What is the total revenue generated?
-What is the average revenue per order?
-How many units were sold?
-How many orders were placed?
-Which day generated the highest sales?
-Which product generated the highest revenue?
-Which region performed best?
-Which payment method was used most frequently?
-What percentage of orders were delivered?
-How many orders are pending or cancelled?
-Which product category generated the most revenue?
-How can sales and order performance be improved?

## Process
Step 1: Data Collection
Collect the retail sales transaction data containing order details, product information, sales amounts, dates, regions, payment methods, and order status.
Step 2: Data Cleaning
Check for missing values, duplicate orders, incorrect dates, inconsistent product names, and invalid numerical values.
Step 3: Data Analysis
Calculate total sales, average sales per order, total quantity, order counts, regional revenue, product-wise revenue, and order status percentages.
Step 4: Data Visualization
Create line charts, bar charts, and doughnut charts to represent sales trends, product performance, regional sales, payment methods, and order status.
Step 5: Dashboard Development
Combine KPI cards, charts, reporting dates, and interactive filters into a single dashboard using Microsoft Excel.



## Project Insight
The following insights are based on the values displayed in your dashboard.
1. Laptop is the top-performing product
Laptop sales reached ₹3,00,000, making it the highest-revenue product in the displayed product chart.
2. Hyderabad leads regional sales
Hyderabad generated ₹1,86,400, followed by Mumbai at ₹1,31,200, Bengaluru at ₹1,30,000, and Chennai at ₹99,000.
3. Sales peaked on 6 September
The daily sales chart shows the highest individual daily value at ₹1,20,000 on 6 September 2026.
4. UPI is the most-used payment method
UPI accounted for 7 orders (46.67%), while Card and Cash each accounted for 4 orders (26.67%).
5. Delivery performance is relatively strong
12 out of 15 orders were delivered, giving an 80% delivery rate.
6. Some orders require follow-up
Two orders were pending (13.33%) and one was cancelled (6.67%). Together, these represent 20% of all orders.
7. Electronics is the leading category
The dashboard reports Electronics sales of ₹3,60,000, indicating that this category contributes substantially to overall revenue.
Management recommendations
Prioritize laptop inventory and promotional campaigns while monitoring profitability.
Study Hyderabad's sales performance to identify practices that could be applied in other regions.
Investigate the reasons for daily sales peaks and use those patterns to inform future campaigns.
Continue supporting UPI payments while retaining other payment options.
Follow up on pending orders and analyze cancellation reasons to improve order fulfillment.


## Final Conclusion
The Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard successfully demonstrates how retail transaction data can be transformed into meaningful business information using data analytics and visualization techniques. It provides a consolidated view of sales revenue, product performance, regional performance, payment preferences, and order fulfillment.

The analysis identifies laptops as the highest-revenue product, Hyderabad as the top-performing region, and UPI as the most frequently used payment method. The dashboard also highlights an 80% delivery rate and identifies pending and cancelled orders as areas for improvement.

Overall, the project demonstrates the practical application of Microsoft Excel in business intelligence and decision-making. It helps management monitor performance, recognize sales opportunities, and develop strategies to improve revenue and operational efficiency.
